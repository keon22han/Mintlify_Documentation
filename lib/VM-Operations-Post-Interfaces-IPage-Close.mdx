## Close\(\) {#VM_Operations_Post_Interfaces_IPage_Close}

Closes [`IPage`](/lib/VM-Operations-Post-Interfaces-IPage) and removes [`IView`](/lib/VM-Operations-Post-Interfaces-IView) objects associated with it.

```csharp
void Close()
```

### Remarks

After this call, `IPage` is no longer included in [GetPages\(\)](/lib/VM-API-Post-Operations-ApplicationHandler-GetPages). The associated `IView` objects are also removed.
