<a id="VM_ViewModels_Post_IHeatMapSTFTView3DViewModel"></a>

Namespace: [VM.ViewModels.Post](/lib/VM-ViewModels-Post)  
Assembly: VM.ViewModels.Post.dll  

Represents the 3D waterfall view of a Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT) heat map, reached through [`STFT3DViewModel`](/lib/VM-Operations-Post-Interfaces-IOperationsHeatMapSTFTViewModel-STFT3DViewModel).

This view shows STFT values as a 3D waterfall surface over time and frequency and is used when [`IsWaterfallType`](/lib/VM-Operations-Post-Interfaces-IOperationsHeatMapSTFTViewModel-IsWaterfallType) is set to show the waterfall instead of the flat 2D map.
